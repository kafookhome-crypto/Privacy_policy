KAFOOK respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how to exercise your rights under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and its implementing regulations issued by the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

1. Data we collect

The app does not require an account, and we collect no directly identifying data beyond what you enter to make a booking:

  • Contact data: your name and Saudi mobile number.
  • Address data: district, full address, and property type (apartment, villa, etc.).
  • Location: coordinates used to auto-fill your district and schedule the visit. Collected only with your explicit permission; you may refuse and enter the district manually.
  • Service details: requested services, appointment date/time, and order total.
  • Order data: order reference and status.
  • Payment data: your card number is never stored by the app and never passes through KAFOOK servers; it is entered directly on the Moyasar payment gateway.

Your order records are also stored locally on your device (Room database) so you can view and track them offline.

2. How we use your data

  • To fulfil bookings, provide the service, and schedule the visit.
  • To contact you by phone and WhatsApp to confirm appointments and share order status.
  • To calculate the price and collect payment via Moyasar, or on-site at the visit.
  • To auto-fill your district and address from your location for faster booking.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

3. Legal basis

We rely on your explicit consent when you submit a booking, on the necessity of performing the service contract, and on compliance with applicable Saudi law.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. It is shared only as necessary with:

5. Location

The app uses the Android location service (Google Play Services) to obtain your coordinates, then resolves them to a district name using the built-in Google geocoder or, as a fallback, Nominatim from OpenStreetMap. Only coordinates are sent — no other address data. You may deny the location permission and type the district manually.

6. Your rights

Under the PDPL you have the right to:

  • Access your data and request a copy of it.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data once it is no longer necessary for the purpose of collection.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Object to the processing of your data.
  • File a complaint with the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

To exercise any of these rights, email kafookhome@gmail.com. We respond within the period required by law.

7. Children's privacy

The app is directed at adult customers of home services, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

8. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure, and we use encrypted connections (HTTPS) when sending data to our servers.

9. Retention

We retain your order data for as long as needed to perform the service and meet legal and accounting obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the effective date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
